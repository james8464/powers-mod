# Faultbound Verdict Ground Slam design

## Intent

Replace Ground Slam's immediate crater loop with a finite, server-owned seismic rite. The result must be readable before impact, terrain-safe by default, bounded under multiplayer load, compatible with Elemental Blast, and explicit about every protection that can stop damage or forced movement.

## Contract

- One rite per caster opens a twelve-tick ochre-violet fault clock at the caster's feet. Motion may carry its centre toward the caster during the omen, but only inside a finite leash and at a capped step.
- The primary verdict resolves once. Communion authors one reduced offset echo six ticks later; Dominion authors one deeper central crown twelve ticks after the primary. A hard global rite cap and exclusive expiry bound all work.
- Each beat resolves a loaded supporting surface before querying bodies. Unsupported air, safe zones, tagged amethyst, and powered wards stop it. Water softens the quake, Darkness hollows it, and Pure Light refracts it; none of those reactions mutate realm matter.
- Bodies are nearest-first and capped. Safe zones, body amethyst, Sanctuary, Kinetic Ward, personal forcefields, projection anchors, time locks, movement consent, and collision are resolved before forbidden damage or velocity writes. Airborne bodies receive only a reduced tremor.
- Might expands damage, radius, caps, and pressure. Motion moves the omen. Insight reveals successfully struck veils. Wardcraft grants a short earthen absorption mantle. Communion adds its reduced echo. Veil's dust shroud clears at most eight visible hostile mob memories once. Dominion adds the crown beat and larger caps.
- Terrain is unchanged unless server policy explicitly enables damage. Even then, only the primary ordinary-earth beat may remove a deterministic, bounded set of soft, non-fluid, non-realm-force blocks; no vanilla explosion is used and no item-drop storm is created.
- Death, disconnect, respawn, dimension change, power loss, suppression, time freeze, expiry, unload, and shutdown clear identifier-only state exactly once.

## Presentation

Ochre fault spokes and violet gravity runes contract during the omen. Each beat emits a low radial fracture, lifted dust glyphs, and a bass impact. Water answers cyan, Darkness answers black-violet, Pure Light answers white-gold, and each ward or body counter receives its own double seal and sound. Rank effects remain visible without concealing counterplay.
