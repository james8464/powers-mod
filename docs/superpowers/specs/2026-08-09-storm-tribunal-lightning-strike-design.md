# Storm Tribunal Lightning Strike design

## Intent

Replace Lightning Strike's same-tick, zero-cooldown fan-out with a finite, server-owned judgment. The result must remain faster and more surgical than Starfall, expose a real warning window, never scan or chain without a cap, respect every protection before forbidden effects, support Elemental Blast ownership, and make each rank branch mechanically legible.

## Contract

- One tribunal per caster opens an eight-tick cyan-gold storm compass over one exact loaded column. A sixty-tick base cooldown prevents energy-cheap invulnerability-frame spam. Recasting while active is refused without replacing state.
- The initial endpoint is finite, within the world border, loaded, and resolved against the column's outermost motion-blocking surface. A roof above the requested height catches the strike at its upper face instead of allowing damage through it. Every warning beat revalidates the live column before Wardcraft or any other effect, so newly created wards, amethyst, protection, or unloaded terrain immediately interrupt it.
- Safe zones, tagged amethyst or powered wards, hostile Sanctuary/Kinetic Ward boundaries, and Darkness stop the column. Water conducts a wider but attenuated verdict; Pure Light amplifies it without mutating either realm force.
- Impact bodies are radial, nearest-first, deterministic, and capped. Safe zones, body amethyst, Sanctuary, and personal forcefields resolve before damage. Vulnerable projection bodies still accept mirrored damage, while projection anchors and time locks prevent them from relaying secondary conduction. Failed damage never grants secondary effects or creates a chain source.
- Exactly one conductive chain grows from the nearest successfully struck wet body admitted by its bounded inspection. Every target is unique, wet, loaded, in range, independently protected, nearest-first within that candidate set, and subject to a global link cap and per-link attenuation. A blocked link visibly terminates the branch and cannot be bypassed. Engine traversal aborts after inspecting 64 typed direct bodies or 32 typed chain/rank entities; eligibility and deterministic distance ordering then run over that fixed set. Pathological crowds can therefore reduce selection rather than extend server work without bound.
- Might increases impact radius, target cap, and damage. Motion tracks an originally aimed living target during the warning under finite step and leash caps. Insight reveals successfully struck veils. Wardcraft grounds a finite set of hostile projectiles crossing the column without changing ownership. Communion adds one reduced fork from the second successful chain node. Veil leaves misleading storm afterimages and clears a bounded set of hostile mob memories only after a successful impact. Dominion authors one reduced crown beat four ticks after the primary.
- Death, disconnect, respawn, dimension change, exact captured source-power loss, suppression, time freeze, expiry, unload, a blocked Dominion crown, and shutdown collapse identifier-only state exactly once without a false completion. A delegated Elemental Blast cast remains owned only while that parent power occupies a slot; owning Lightning Strike cannot substitute for it, or vice versa.

## Presentation

The omen is an eight-spoked cyan compass crossed by contracting gold rings, a visible sky filament, and ascending electric glyphs. The primary verdict layers vanilla visual-only lightning with a cyan-white ribbon column, branching sparks, ground fracture, thunder, and a ritual clash. Water answers with rippling blue circuitry; Pure Light answers white-gold; Darkness swallows the filament in black-violet. Every ward, protected body, roof, resistance, interruption, chain link, rank fork, afterimage, and crown has a dedicated semantic seal and sound.
